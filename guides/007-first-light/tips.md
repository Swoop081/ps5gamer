# Hints, tips & cheats

## Hints
- There are 17 chapter-select entries. About 9 to 11 are real playable missions (sources differ), the rest are story interludes.
- The opening tutorial missions run around 3 hours.
- Main story is around 20 hours.
- 92 collectibles in total (playing cards, mementos, postcards and more). Use the Push Square collectibles guide.
- Learn Spycraft and Instinct early, they are used in every mission.
