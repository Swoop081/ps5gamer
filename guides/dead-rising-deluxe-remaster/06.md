# Case 5 (Day 3, midnight)
## 5-1 A Promise to Isabela (12am to 1am, Sept 21)
1. Be at the marked abandoned store in North Plaza by midnight. Until then you can rescue survivors or hunt PP stickers.
2. Isabela arrives with a bullet wound. Kill the zombie chasing her.
3. Pick her up.

## 5-2 Transporting Isabela (12am to 3am)
1. Carry Isabela on your back to the Security Room. Zombies attack you less while you carry her.
2. Use the Wonderland Plaza to Paradise Plaza shortcut, the quickest and safest route.
3. A cutscene with Brad and Jessie ends the case. You then have a few hours before Isabela wakes.
