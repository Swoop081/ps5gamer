# Infinity Mode and Platinum
- **Infinity Mode** unlocks after Overtime Mode. Health slowly drains and food is limited. Save Suspension lets you back it up.
- Platinum: about **20 to 25 hours**, roughly 4/10 difficulty, **51 trophies** (1 Platinum, 3 Gold, 2 Silver, 45 Bronze), no online trophies.
- Trophy examples: photograph at least 4 psychopaths, photograph all PP Stickers, change into at least 5 costumes.
