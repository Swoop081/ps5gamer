# Psychopaths and boss tactics
12 psychopaths count for trophies (10 optional, 2 tied to cases). Photograph at least 4 of them (dead or alive) for Psycho Photo.

## Carlito (Case 1-2, Food Court)
- Cross under the welcome sign, dodging grenades. Climb the boxes by the Great Big Burger sign and vault onto his balcony.
- Fight from a corner with the Handgun or Submachine Gun. He goes down in a few hits. Bring healing items.

## Carlito (Case 2-2, Entrance Plaza)
- Use the Mini Chainsaws from Adam or the Submachine Gun. Take the escalator up to his floor.
- He has a sniper rifle and each hit costs two health blocks. Go left so Brad draws fire, move between stone pillars and learn the gap between his shots.

## Steven (Case 2-3, Seon's)
- Buy Katanas next door at Ripper's Blades. Steven charges down aisles with a knife-covered trolley, so stay near an aisle end or on shelving.
- When he draws his shotgun, climb the shelves, drop behind him and slash. Retreat over the shelves, drop on his other side, repeat. Healing items are in the store.

## Isabela (Case 4-2, North Plaza)
- She rides a motorcycle, so bring guns. Camp on the top scaffolding (she can't ride there) and shoot. Jump aside from head-on charges.
- **Do not leave the arena.** Entering a store or any area with a loading screen fails the case. Healing is on the scaffolding and Ripper's Blades has more weapons.

## Larry the Butcher (Case 8-4)
- Carlito is being dragged toward the meat grinder, so move quickly. Bring shotguns (Huntin' Shack) or the submachine gun above the Warehouse door.
- Stay at range. He turns invisible briefly after each hit. Dodge thrown carcasses (leave loose ones between you and him), cleaver swings and the ram charge. It takes many hits, so be patient.

## Paul (Long Haired Punk)
A few hits. Put him out with a fire extinguisher afterward to save him.
