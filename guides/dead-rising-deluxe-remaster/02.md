# Case 1 (Day 1, 12pm)
Four missions, no real time pressure until the end.

## 1-1 A Dangerous Encounter
1. Start a new game. Fly in, land on the mall roof, meet Carlito, and get into the Security Room.
2. Look at the coffee cup in the Janitor's Room (cutscene), then go down the long corridor to the Entrance Plaza.
3. Stack objects on the barricade and photograph the 11 people there. The cutscene that follows is scripted, so you can't save them.
4. Follow the man in the yellow shirt upstairs. Meet Brad, Jessie and Otis in the Security Room and photograph them.
5. Take the map and transceiver from Otis, crawl through the vent to the roof, and help Natalie and Jeff Meyer.
6. Ride the elevator to the Warehouse, talk to Otis on the radio, then head down the corridor to Paradise Plaza.

## 1-2 Backup for Brad
1. Go to the Food Court in the southwest corner. The corridor from Paradise Plaza to Entrance Plaza is shuttered, so go north and out by Jill's Sandwiches into Leisure Park.
2. Otis will call about the scoop **Cut from the Same Cloth**. It has a short time limit, so do it first.
3. In Entrance Plaza, grab the **Submachine Gun** on the ledge above Kid's Choice Clothing (stairs left of the entrance, past the big teddy bear).
4. Back in Leisure Park, clear the crowd at the Food Court entrance with the **lawnmower**.
5. Enter the Food Court and fight **Carlito** (boss).

## 1-3 An Odd Old Man
1. Escort Brad back to the Security Room after Carlito escapes.
2. Save at Flexin' in Al Fresca Plaza (first store on your right). Fight through the horde with Brad.
3. Brad lifts the shutter. Keep following him through Entrance Plaza until the Barnaby cutscene.

## 1-4 A Temporary Agreement
1. Go back to the Security Room through Al Fresca Plaza and the Food Court, then Leisure Park.
2. In Al Fresca Plaza pick up **Leah Stein** from Riverfield Jewelry and carry her to the Security Room. This finishes the case and the scoop **A Mother's Lament**.

Next case starts at **6am Sept 20**. Use the free time for scoops.
