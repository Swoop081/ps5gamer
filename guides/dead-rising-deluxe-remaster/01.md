# Before you start
The story is a **72-hour clock** inside Willamette Parkview Mall, from **12pm Sept 19 (Day 1)** to **12pm Sept 22 (Day 4)**. Cases are timed, and failing one can lock you out of the rest, so save often.

**How it works**
- **Cases** are the story missions: 8 cases made up of 20 missions, plus the final bonus case "The Facts".
- **Scoops** are optional survivor and photo side quests. Otis radios you about them.
- Most cases start with a cutscene when you are in the **Security Room** at the right time. Between cases you have free time.
- Survivors you escort back to the Security Room count toward trophies (10 for Humanist, 50 for Saint, and the mall has 48 to rescue).

**Good habits**
- Stock up on healing items and a few strong weapons before any boss fight (called Psychopaths).
- The shortcut from **Wonderland Plaza to Paradise Plaza** (a roof hatch in the women's bathroom, opened in the Out of Control scoop) saves lots of time.
- Do scoops in the free time between cases, and pair them up so you escort several survivors per trip.
