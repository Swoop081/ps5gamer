# Before you start
- The story runs on a **72-hour in-game clock** (Sept 19 to Sept 22 at the mall). Cases are timed, so don't dawdle.
- **Cases** are the main story missions (8 of them). **Scoops** are optional survivor side quests.
- To unlock **Overtime Mode** you must finish all 8 cases and be at the **Helipad above the Security Room at 12:00 PM on Sept 22 (Day 4)**.
- Tip: back up progress with Save Suspension where you can, and level up early by killing zombies and rescuing survivors.

Full case-by-case walkthrough: [Push Square guide](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/dead-rising-1-guide-walkthrough-all-collectibles-and-survivors)
