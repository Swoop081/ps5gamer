# Ending A and Overtime Mode
- Finish **all 8 cases**, visit Isabela, then wait at the **helipad at noon on Sept 22**. That gives **Ending A** and unlocks **Overtime Mode**.
- Finish Overtime Mode to get the **True Ending** (also called Ending S).
- Other endings (B to F) come from missing cases or the helicopter. Letting time run out during the Bomb Collector case gives the darkest one (Ending F).
