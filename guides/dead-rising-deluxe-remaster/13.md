# The Facts, Ending A and Overtime
## The Facts - Memories (10pm to 11pm, Sept 21)
1. After beating Larry, take a truck from outside the Meat Processing Area and drive to **Carlito's Hideout** in North Plaza (through Leisure Park). Arrive by **10pm**.
2. Check the Case File. All cases should show closed. Answer Jessie's call and watch the cutscenes.
3. Go to the Security Room and kill **Jessie** when she returns as a zombie (she eats the Special Forces soldiers who arrive).
4. Special Forces patrol the whole mall afterward and are tough, so move carefully in your free time.

## Ending A
- **10am Sept 22:** be at Carlito's Hideout to talk to Isabela.
- **12pm Sept 22:** be on the **Helipad above the Security Room**. That gives **Ending A** and unlocks **Overtime Mode**.

## All endings
- **S:** finish Overtime Mode (the true ending).
- **A:** all cases done, talk to Isabela at 10am, helipad at 12pm.
- **B:** stop doing cases before Case 7-1 and go to the helipad.
- **C:** all cases done but ignore Isabela's 10am call.
- **D:** get captured by Special Forces and fail to break free (mash the left stick) before the helicopter leaves.
- **E:** miss cases and miss the helipad at noon.
- **F:** run out of time during Bomb Collector.
