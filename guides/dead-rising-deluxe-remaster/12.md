# Day 3 Scoops (Sept 21)
**16. A Strange Group** (12am to 10am). Colby's Movieland, Paradise Plaza. Kill the cult members, avoiding their smoke (it captures you). In screen 4 you meet and beat **Sean** the cult leader. Take the Movieland Warehouse Key (door left of the stage) and escort **Beth Shrake, Cheryl Jones, Michelle Feltz, Nathan Crabbe and Ray Mathison**.

**17. Floyd the Sommelier** (early Day 3). Only if you saved Floyd. He wants wine to calm the other survivors. Bring some to the Security Room.

**18. Long Haired Punk** (5am to 5pm). Casual Gals, Wonderland Plaza. Fight **Paul** (he goes down in a few hits). He sets himself on fire afterward, so grab a **fire extinguisher** and put him out to turn him into a survivor. Then save him, **Debbie Willet and Mindy Baker** (they hide in a storage cupboard).

**19. A Sick Man** (8am to 4pm). Estelle's Fine-lady Cosmetics, Wonderland Plaza. Keep talking to **Leroy McKenna** until he follows. He can walk himself.

**20. Photographer's Pride** (noon, Paradise Plaza). Be in front of Colombian Roastmasters **before 12:05**. Kent has turned hostile and is holding **Tad Hawthorne** hostage. Stop him, kill Kent, untie Tad and bring him to the Security Room. Reward: the Camera 2 book. Arrive late and Kent strips you of your items and clothes, and you fight him with only what's around the fountain outside Jill's Sandwiches.

**21. Kindell's Betrayal** (afternoon, deadline 5pm). Needs Kindell Johnson rescued (North Plaza corridor toward Crislip's, approach him closely) and at least three other survivors in the Security Room. Talk Kindell out of leading everyone out, and persuade him to wait until after noon on Sept 22. Don't leave the room while it's active. Needed for the Transmissionary trophy.

**22. A Woman in Despair** (12pm to 5pm). Players, Paradise Plaza. **Simone Ravendark** only joins if Isabela is in the Security Room.

**23. Paul's Present** (about 5pm, Security Room). Only if you saved Paul. Talk to him and he'll give you Molotov cocktails on demand. Stock up early, because supply stops once the survivors leave.

**24. Simone the Gunslinger** (evening). Only if you saved Simone. She asks you to bring her a handgun.

**25. Cheryl's Request** (about 9pm). Only if you saved Cheryl. Find her in the room with the blue door and take a photo of her. One photo is enough. Counts toward Transmissionary.
