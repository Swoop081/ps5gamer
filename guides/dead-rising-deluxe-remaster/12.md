# Day 3 Scoops (Sept 21)
**16. A Strange Group** (12am to 10am). Where: Colby's Movieland, Paradise Plaza. Kill the cult members, avoiding their smoke (it captures you). In screen 4 you meet and beat **Sean** the cult leader. Take the Movieland Warehouse Key (door left of the stage) and escort **Beth Shrake, Cheryl Jones, Michelle Feltz, Nathan Crabbe and Ray Mathison**.

**17. Floyd the Sommelier** (early Day 3, only if you saved Floyd). Floyd asks for wine to calm the other survivors. Bring some to the Security Room.

**18. Long Haired Punk** (5am to 5pm). Where: Casual Gals, Wonderland Plaza. Fight **Paul**. He sets himself on fire after losing, so grab a **fire extinguisher** and put him out to turn him into a survivor. Then save him, **Debbie Willet and Mindy Baker**.

**19. A Sick Man** (8am to 4pm). Where: Estelle's Fine-lady Cosmetics, Wonderland Plaza. Keep talking to **Leroy McKenna** until he follows. He can walk by himself.

**20. Photographer's Pride** (around noon). Kent's second challenge, in Paradise Plaza. It comes with no reminder call, so don't miss it.

**21. Kindell's Betrayal.** Details not written yet, see the [full list](https://pushsquare.com/guides/dead-rising-1-all-cases-and-scoops).

**22. A Woman in Despair** (12pm to 5pm). Where: Players, Paradise Plaza. **Simone Ravendark** only joins if Isabela is in the Security Room (depends on case progress).

**23. Paul's Present.** Details not written yet.

**24. Simone the Gunslinger** (evening). Only if you saved Simone. She asks you to bring her a handgun.

**25. Cheryl's Request.** Details not written yet.
