# Day 2 Scoops (Sept 20)
**7. Lovers** (7am to 12pm). Where: Run Like the Wind, Wonderland Plaza 2F. Talk to Tonya and Ross until they follow. Carry **Ross Folk**. **Do not give Ross a gun** when he asks, or he will die.

**8. The Hatchet Man** (8am to 3pm). Where: Crislip's Home Saloon, North Plaza. Beat **Cliff** (psychopath), take the Empty Store Key from his wallet, unlock the metal doors on your right, and escort **Barbara Patterson, Josh Manning and Rich Atkins**.

**9. The Coward** (9am to 3pm). Where: McHandy's Hardware, Al Fresca Plaza. **Gordon Stalworth** won't respond to talking. Throw a cardboard box at him to make him join.

**10. Restaurant Man** (11am to 4pm). Where: Jill's Sandwiches, Paradise Plaza. Kick in a side window. **Ronald Shiner** only joins if you give him food, so carry some.

**11. Above the Law** (12pm to midnight). Where: Lovely Fashion House, Wonderland Plaza. Stock up and fight **Jo the Cop** for the Handcuff Key. Free **Kay Nelson, Lily Deacon, Kelly Carpenter and Janet Star**. Take the shortcut home.

**12. The Woman Who Didn't Make It** (5pm to midnight). Where: Gramma's Kids, Entrance Plaza. Do **Mark of the Sniper** first. Find **Jolie Wu**, take her to For Her upstairs to meet **Rachel Decker**, and escort both.

**13. Mark of the Sniper** (5pm to midnight). Where: Entrance Plaza. Three snipers on the second floor. Isolate one and kill him up close for the Sniper Rifle, use it on the others, then find **Wayne Blackwell** in Estelle's Fine-lady Cosmetics. Use the pillars as cover.

**14. Antique Lover** (5pm to midnight). Where: Ned's Knicknackery, Entrance Plaza 2F. After the snipers are dead, talk to **Floyd Sanders** in the back corner until he joins. Combine this with scoops 12 and 13 for four survivors in one trip.

**15. Ronald's Appetite** (Day 2 evening, in the Security Room). Needs Ronald rescued and at least three other survivors in the room. Ronald starts a mutiny to go find food. **Give him food** to calm him down. Don't leave the Security Room while it's active or Ronald's group vanishes.
