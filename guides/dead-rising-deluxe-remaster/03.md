# Case 2 (Day 2, 6am to 11am)
## 2-1 Image in the Monitor
Be in the Security Room at **6am on Sept 20**. It is just a cutscene.

## 2-2 Rescue the Professor (7am to 11am)
1. Leave for Paradise Plaza. Otis radios that the Entrance Plaza shutter is open.
2. Bring healing items and both close and long range weapons.
3. In the Entrance Plaza, **Carlito** is back for another boss fight with Barnaby hanging above the zombies. Focus on Carlito, not the crowd.
4. Afterward Brad is hurt, which leads straight into the next mission.

## 2-3 Medicine Run (expires 11am)
1. Head to **Seon's Food & Stuff** in the northwest corner of North Plaza. If you have the Out of Control shortcut, use it (roof hatch in the Paradise Plaza women's bathroom).
2. Stock up first. Ripper's Blades is next door.
3. Inside, **Steven** (psychopath, charges with a knife and tool trolley) waits. Reach the doors between the seafood and meat counters to trigger him.
4. Beat Steven for the **Pharmacy Key**, go through the door at the end of the corridor, and take the **First Aid Kit**.
5. Bring it to the Security Room and talk to Jessie in the Janitor's Room.

You then have free time before Case 3.
