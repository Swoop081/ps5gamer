# Case 4 (Day 2, 3pm)
## 4-1 Another Source
Be in the Security Room at **3pm on Sept 20**. Cutscene only. It unlocks Case 4-2.

## 4-2 Girl Hunting (3pm to midnight)
1. Go after the woman Steven had hostage in Medicine Run. From the Security Room use the Paradise Plaza to Wonderland Plaza shortcut.
2. Pick up the **Submachine Gun** in Paradise Plaza on the way. You may stumble on a cult sacrificing a survivor there.
3. Go to North Plaza outside Seon's Food & Stuff and follow the purple question mark.
4. The woman is revealed to be **Isabela**, and you fight her while she rides a motorcycle. Beating her ends the case.
