# Day 1 Scoops (Sept 19)
Each scoop: tick the chapter once you've done the ones you want.

**1. Cut from the Same Cloth** (2pm to 8pm). Where: Colombian Roastmasters, Entrance Plaza 2F. Meet photographer **Kent Swanson**. Photograph him with his camera raised, then crouching downstairs (500 PP), then fist-pumping (700 PP, include zombies in the shot to boost the score). Unlocks the Photo Challenge scoop.

**2. Barricade Pair** (4pm to 4am). Where: Weber's Garments, Al Fresca Plaza. Clear the barricade, enter, and survive Burt's attack using a light melee weapon (like a bat) until he calms down. Escort **Aaron Swoop and Burt Thompson** back.

**3. A Mother's Lament** (5pm to 7am). Where: Riverfield Jewelry, Al Fresca Plaza. Talk to **Leah Stein** until she joins, then carry her (injured leg) to the Security Room. Doing this also completes Case 1-4.

**4. Out of Control** (9pm to 11am). Where: Wonderland Plaza. Use the control panel at the second-floor entrance station to trigger **Adam the Clown**. After he's dead, use the panel in the coaster station, free **Greg Simpson**, clear the way, and use his shortcut (women's bathroom roof hatch) to Paradise Plaza.

**5. Japanese Tourists** (10pm to 7am). Where: Sir Book-a-Lot, Wonderland Plaza. The two men can't understand you. Pick up the book **Japanese Conversation** by the register, talk again, and escort **Yuu Tanaka and Shinji Kitano**. Combine with Out of Control.

**6. Shadow of the North Plaza** (11pm to 8am). Where: purple question mark store in North Plaza. Talk to **David Bailey** several times, then carry him back (use the shortcut).
