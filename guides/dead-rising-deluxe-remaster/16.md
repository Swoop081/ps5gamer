# 100% Completion Roadmap (Platinum)
51 trophies (1 Platinum, 3 Gold, 2 Silver, 45 Bronze), no online trophies, no glitched ones, difficulty doesn't matter. Expect 20 to 25 hours and about two playthroughs plus Infinity Mode. **16 trophies are missable.** Hardest: Saint.

## Step 1 (optional but recommended): Zombie Annihilator
- Kill **53,594** zombies. Kills carry over between playthroughs, so you can do it any time, but doing it first gets you close to Level 50 and the Real Mega Buster (300 shots, one-headshot kills on regular zombies).
- Method 1 (~4 hours, no prep): drive vehicles through the Maintenance Tunnels in a loop. About 2,500 to 3,000 kills per cycle, 15 to 20 cycles.
- Method 2 (~3 hours): after rescuing 50 survivors and finishing Overtime, use a Rocket Launcher with the Infinite Ammo Magazine in the caves, where the timer stops.

## Step 2: Saint and True Ending run
- Complete all 8 cases and The Facts. Be at the helipad at 12pm on Sept 22 for Overtime, then beat its final boss for the True Ending and Infinity Mode.
- **Saint** = 50 survivors in one playthrough. Frank doesn't count. Isabela only counts if you finish Overtime. Skip PP stickers and costumes on this run, time is tight.
- If one or two survivors die, reload an earlier save.
- 50 survivors unlocks the Infinite Ammo/Durability magazine.

## Step 3: The Grateful Eight and clean-up
- **The Grateful Eight and Tour Guide:** escort 8 female survivors at once (around 9am to 1pm on Day 2, using scoops and fast-forwarding). Only 8 survivors can spawn at once. Rescuing Jennifer finishes the Raincoat Cult sequence.
- Then PP stickers, costumes and the remaining trophies.

## Step 4: Infinity Mode (7 Day Survivor)
- Prep first: Zombie Annihilator and Saint done, Infinite Ammo magazine and Real Mega Buster from the Security Room, Level 50.
- Collect the Health 1, Health 2 and Survival magazines (together they triple food healing).
- Fast-forward time at bathrooms. Allow about 90 minutes real time. Use Save Suspension for backups.

## Remaining trophies
- **Self Defense / Punisher:** kill 1 / 10 psychopaths (missable). **Psycho Photo:** photograph 4 psychopaths (missable).
- **Legendary Soldier:** kill 10 special forces (from Day 4). **Karate Champ:** 30 barehanded kills (special moves count).
- **Hella Copter:** destroy the helicopter in Leisure Park during Overtime (Real Mega Buster one-shots it).
- **Carjacker:** kill the Convicts (6pm Leisure Park) and take their vehicle (missable).
- **No, Thank You:** 100 raincoat cultists, 1pm Day 1 to 1pm next day (or until Sean dies). Must be in one playthrough.
- **Humanist / Saint:** 10 / 50 survivors out. **Full Set:** all 78 notebook portraits.
- **Portraiture:** photograph 10 survivors (missable). **Census Taker:** 30 unique survivors (missable).
- **The Artiste:** 10,000 PP from one photo (missable). Kent's Camera 2 book doubles photo PP if you kill him during Photographer's Pride.
- **Group Photo:** 50 target markers in one shot (easy at the Entrance Plaza gate or crowded tunnels).
- **PP Photo / PP Collector:** 10 sticker types / all 100 stickers.
- **Sharp Dresser / Clothes Horse:** 5 costumes / all 46 mall costumes.
- **Juice Freak:** make all 7 blender juices. **Item Smasher:** smash 30 items. **Bullet Point:** fire 300 bullets.
- **Transmissionary:** answer 30 of 42 radio calls (don't carry over between playthroughs).
- **Road Rage:** 500 zombies with vehicles. **Freefall:** drop 16 ft. **Marathon Runner:** 42.195 km on foot. **Strike!:** 5 zombies with one bowling ball. **Costume Party:** 10 masks on zombies. **Raining Zombies:** 10 parasol knockdowns.
- **Rampage:** 100 rocket launcher kills. **Zombie Road:** ride 10 m on zombies. **Stunt Rider:** jump a bike 10 m (North Plaza ramp). **Nice Shot:** golf ball 100 m.
- **Mall Worker:** follow Greg to the secret passage in Out of Control (missable).
- **Snuff Shot B:** meet Brad in the Maintenance Tunnels Warehouse after Case 7-2 (missable). **Snuff Shot J:** return to the Security Room after The Facts to meet Jessie, before the midnight arrival of the special forces (missable).
- **Overtime Mode, Infinity Mode, Never Give Up, Level Max (50), 3/5/7 Day Survivor:** story and progress trophies.
