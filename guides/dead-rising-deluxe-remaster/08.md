# Case 7 (Day 3, 11am to 5pm)
## 7-1 The Last Resort
Be in the Security Room by **11am on Sept 21**. The cutscene reveals Carlito's bombs in the Maintenance Tunnels. (One guide says 12pm, but the start time is 11am, so be early.)

## 7-2 Bomb Collector (11am to 5pm)
This is the case that gives **Ending F** if time runs out.
1. Go to the car icon in the northwest corner of Leisure Park (between Wonderland Plaza and North Plaza).
2. Take the **motorcycle**, the first vehicle in the outside car park. Use it rather than the bathroom entrances.
3. In the Maintenance Tunnels go **counter-clockwise** around the edge, usually turning right at junctions.
4. Find **5 bombs** in trucks at the ends of the paths. Open the back doors of each and examine to disarm it.
5. Carlito drives his own truck and tries to knock you off. Ignore him and keep disarming.
6. Return to the tunnel entrance. A cutscene shows Brad trapped, and you are sent back to the Security Room.
