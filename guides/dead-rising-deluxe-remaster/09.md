# Case 8 (Day 3, 5pm to 10pm)
## 8-1 Jamming Device (5pm to 6pm)
Be in the Security Room at **5pm on Sept 21**. Isabela explains Carlito has a hideout with a signal jammer. Cutscene only.

## 8-2 Hideout (5pm to 10pm)
1. Open your map and go to the abandoned store on the hallway to Huntin' Shack.
2. Follow **Isabela**. She picks the route and doesn't follow you, so help by clearing zombies in her path, through Paradise Plaza and Leisure Park.
3. Fight through the horde in North Plaza to **Carlito's hideout** and approach Isabela at the laptop.

## 8-3 Jessie's Discovery
1. Before leaving, grab **shotguns** from Huntin' Shack (next to the hideout).
2. Hurry back to the Security Room where Jessie is calling you. Cutscene only. You must still fit in Case 8-4 before **10pm**.

## 8-4 The Butcher
1. In Leisure Park take the northwest path to the Maintenance Tunnels. Grab a **motorcycle** from the car park.
2. Turn left and follow the road to the **Meat Processing Area**.
3. Defeat **Larry the Butcher** (psychopath) inside. A cutscene ends the case.
