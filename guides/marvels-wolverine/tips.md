# Hints, tips & cheats

## Hints
- Linear game: 30 main missions, no open world. About 15 hours for a normal run.
- Any mission can be replayed from the Nightmare Cabin, handy for cleaning up collectibles and trophies.
- 20 whisky bottles to find, and Material Crates for crafting suits.
- Max your techniques before the Essex fights.
- Nightmare Trials are optional extra challenges.
